Projects

What we're actually working on.

Not a portfolio — we haven't earned one. This is the work in front of us right now, with its real status attached. Where a project needs data we don't have, that's written down too, and it's an open invitation.

Building

A tidy pipeline for camera-trap data

The unglamorous part everyone rebuilds: read the images, keep the timestamps and station metadata straight, deduplicate bursts, and produce a table an ecologist can work with. Open from the start.

Needs: a couple of real, messy folders to test against

Exploring

Bioacoustic identification where coverage is thin

Existing classifiers are strong for well-recorded regions and weak elsewhere. We're reading the literature and the open datasets to find where a small, carefully-trained model would help most.

Needs: recordings and local knowledge from an under-covered region

Planned

Canopy change from open satellite imagery

Sentinel and Landsat archives are free; turning them into a dated record of loss and regrowth for one chosen watershed is a well-defined problem and a good first public artefact.

Needs: a watershed worth watching, and someone who knows it on the ground
