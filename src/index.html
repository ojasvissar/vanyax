What we do

Five kinds of field data, read properly

Field teams collect far more than they can read. Recorders fill drives that are never opened; camera-trap folders sit untouched between grants. We build the models that read it.

Bioacoustics

An autonomous recorder captures more sound in a season than a person could listen to in a year. We build the classifiers that find the species in it — including where existing models barely reach.

Camera traps

Millions of frames, most of them empty or set off by wind. We clear the empties, deduplicate bursts, tag what is left and keep timestamps and station data straight.

Surveys & counts

Transects, point counts, ringing records, plot surveys — often the longest series anyone holds. We turn them into occupancy and abundance models you can defend.

Satellite & drone

Open Sentinel and Landsat archives already cover your site. We turn them, and your own drone flights, into dated maps of canopy loss, regrowth and change.

eDNA pipelines

From sequencing reads to a species list: the pipeline, the reference database, and an honest line on what the reads can support. In development.
