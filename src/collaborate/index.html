Collaborate

You do the fieldwork. We'll do the analysis.

This page is for anyone gathering data about ecosystems without a data scientist on hand — rangers, field biologists, community monitoring groups, small NGOs, conservancies, students. You don't need to know what model you want. You need to know what you collected and what you're trying to find out.

What to send

Messy is fine. Incomplete is fine. Most ecological data is both, and part of the job is dealing with that.

  • Audio from passive recorders — hours, nights or years of it
  • Camera-trap images, tagged or untagged
  • Transect counts, point counts, plot surveys, ringing records
  • eDNA or metabarcoding reads
  • GPS tracks and telemetry
  • Satellite or drone imagery over a site you care about
  • Spreadsheets kept by hand over many seasons

If it isn't on this list, ask anyway. The categories matter less than whether the question can be answered with what you have.

What happens

  1. A first message

    Write in plain language: what you have, how it was collected, and what decision you'd make differently if you knew the answer.

  2. An honest scope

    We come back with what's answerable, what isn't, and the smallest first step. If statistics or a well-made chart will do, we'll tell you that instead of building a model.

  3. A small sample first

    Before anything large moves, we work on a slice — enough to prove the pipeline runs and the labels mean what we think they mean.

  4. The build

    Code you can read, a model you can run, and a written account of what it does badly as well as well.

  5. Handover

    You get the outputs, the code and the documentation. We ask to publish only the general parts, and only if you agree.

Your data

Conservation data can put animals at risk. These rules aren't boilerplate.

You own it. We don't claim rights over anything you send, and we don't reuse it for other work without asking.

Sensitive locations stay out of public outputs. Precise coordinates for anything poachable or collectable are generalised or withheld in everything we publish.

We'll sign your agreement. If your institution, community or funder needs a data-sharing agreement or an ethics review, send it — that's normal.

We say when a model isn't the answer. Plenty of ecological questions are answered better by careful counting than by machine learning, and pretending otherwise wastes your season.
