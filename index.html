Work with us
01

See the canopy
from orbit.

Sentinel-2 and Landsat passes, segmented tile by tile into cover, gap and regrowth. We date every loss event to the fortnight, attach it to a parcel, and push the alert to whoever can walk out and check it.

  • U-Net segmentation
  • Cloud-gap infill
  • Parcel-level alerts
02

Hear the forest
all night.

A recorder on a post returns ten thousand hours nobody has time to play back. Our classifiers read the spectrogram instead — species, call type, confidence — and hand back a night's roll call by morning.

  • Spectrogram CNNs
  • Few-shot for rare calls
  • On-device inference
03

Map who needs
whom.

Camera traps, pollinator counts and eDNA reads, resolved into one interaction network. Pull a node and the model shows you what falls over with it — the argument a land manager can actually take to a meeting.

  • Graph inference
  • Metabarcoding pipelines
  • Extinction cascades
