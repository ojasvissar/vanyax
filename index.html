Data science & machine learning for biodiversity

Most ecological data never gets analysed.

Field teams collect far more than they can read: recordings, camera-trap images, transects, samples, rasters. vanyax exists for the other half of that work — the analysis, the models, and the plain answer at the end of it.

What we do

Four things, and nothing else yet.

01 — For people with data

We analyse what you've already collected

You know the ecology and the site. We handle the cleaning, the statistics and the modelling, and give the result back in language you can put in a report.

02 — For people with a question

We build the model the question needs

Species identification from audio or images, occupancy and abundance, change detection, forecasting. Scoped small, and handed over as something you can run without us.

03 — For everyone

We publish what isn't yours

Anything general — model weights, pipelines, notebooks — goes out under a permissive licence with the training details written down. Your data and your sites stay yours.

04 — On our own time

We run our own projects

Where the data is already open, we build things nobody has asked for yet, and say honestly how far along each one is.

How a collaboration goes

Four steps, usually a few weeks. If a model isn't the right answer, we say so at step two and you've lost nothing.

Start one →

  1. You tell us what you have

    A description is enough to begin — what was collected, how, over what period, and what you wish you knew.

  2. We write back with an honest read

    What your data can support, what it can't, and the smallest useful thing to try first. Sometimes that's a spreadsheet and a chart rather than a model.

  3. We build the first version

    Working code and a result, with the limitations stated. You see the messy version, not a polished demo.

  4. You keep it

    Code, weights, documentation and the data you gave us — yours. We publish only the general parts, and only with your agreement.

Where we are

We're at the beginning, and we'd rather say so.

There's no client list on this site and no metrics, because there is nothing yet to count. What there is: the technical skill set, time set aside for a small number of first collaborations, and an intention to publish what comes out of them.

If that's too early for you, that's a fair call. If it isn't — if you have data sitting unanalysed and no one to look at it — this is a good moment to talk, because you'll get our full attention.
