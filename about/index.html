About

Van is the forest. Yax are the spirits that keep it.

The name sets the terms: the forest first, and the work in service of it. vanyax is a data science practice for biodiversity and ecosystems — new, small, and deliberately plain about both.

Why

The gap in conservation isn't only funding or fieldwork. It's that data keeps being collected by people who have no capacity to analyse it. Recorders fill drives that are never opened. Camera-trap folders sit untouched between grants. Surveys are typed into spreadsheets and read once.

Meanwhile the methods that would read all of it are ordinary now — classification, occupancy modelling, change detection — just not ordinary in the places doing the counting. That mismatch is the whole reason for this practice.

How we work

  • The simplest method that answers the question, not the most impressive one
  • Say when machine learning is the wrong tool, before any money or season is spent
  • Write down what a model does badly, in the same place as what it does well
  • Leave every collaborator able to run the thing without us
  • Publish the general parts; protect the sensitive ones
  • No fabricated numbers, on this site or in a report

Who

vanyax is a one-person practice at the moment, run by a data scientist working with ecologists and field teams, and bringing in collaborators as projects need them.

If you'd like to know who you'd be working with before sending anything, ask — you'll get a straight answer and a call.
